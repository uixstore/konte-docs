# Footer Instagram

At the footer, you can display the Instagram photos feed. You just need to enable it in the Customizer then setup this feed in **Customize > Footer > Footer Instagram**.

> [!WARNING] Remember to set the **Instagram Access Token** in **General > API Keys** section first. Without this access toke, the Instagram feed won't show up.


## Enable Footer Instagram


By default, this Instagram feeed section is disabled. You need to enable it in **Customize > Footer > Footer Layout**.


## Instagram Display

You can change how many photos will be displayed and the columns to display them in **Customize > Footer > Footer Instagram**.


## Get Instagram Access Token

You can follow this tutorial to generate your personal Instagram Access Token. This token is required to help the theme connect to your Instagram account and display photos on your websites.

https://tools.uix.store/instagram-access-token/
