# Footer Widgets

Konte allows you to display widgets at footer in columns. All settiongs for footer widgets can be found in **Customize > Footer > Footer Widgets**.


## Enable Footer Widgets


![Enable footer widgets](_images/footer--widgets-enable.png#alignright)

By default, this footer widgets section is disabled. You need to enable it in **Customize > Footer > Footer Layout**.


## Footer Widgets Columns

![Footer widgets columns](_images/customize-footer--widgets.png#alignright)

You can change the footer columns in **Customize > Footer > Footer Widgets**, from 2 to 4 columns.


## Add Widgets to Footer

![Footer widgets sidebars](_images/footer--widgets-sidebars.png#alignright)

After enabled Footer Widgets, you can navigate to **Appearance > Widgets** or **Appearance > Customize > Widgets** to add widgets to footer sidebars. They are sidebars:

- **Footer Sidebar 1**
- **Footer Sidebar 2**
- **Footer Sidebar 3**
- **Footer Sidebar 4**
