## Installing & Configuring WPML

To get started with Konte and WPML, you will need:

- WPML Multilingual CMS (the core plugin)
- String Translation
- Media Translation

With WPML and Konte, you can translate pages, posts, flex posts, portfolio, and Konte's elements added to any page or post, using WPBakery Page Builder or Elementor.


## Translate Your Content Automatically


With WPML Automatic Translation, you have two options for translating your site: **Translate Everything** or **Translate Some**. The WPML setup wizard will try to choose the best option for you depending on how much content is on your site.

- **Translate Everything**: Automatically translate your entire site’s content. This is the quickest way to get your site translated. See this [documentation](https://wpml.org/documentation/automatic-translation/#translating-your-whole-site-automatically) for more details.
- **Translate Some**: Choose which content to translate. If you don’t want to translate your whole site or would prefer to hire translators to work on your site, this option gives you more control what you translate and who will translate it. See this [documentation](https://wpml.org/documentation/automatic-translation/#translating-individual-pages-posts-and-other-content-automatically)


## Translate Your Site Yourself

If you have a WPML Blog account, or you’re the only one translating your site’s content, you can translate your posts and pages by clicking the plus and pencil icons. Please see this [documentation](https://wpml.org/documentation/getting-started-guide/#translating-your-site-yourself) for more details.


## Translating Texts from Theme Options

Besides content coming from posts and pages on your site, there are usually some texts coming from other places. The most common examples are themes and plugins. To learn how to translate these texts, visit WPML’s documentation page about [translating strings](https://wpml.org/documentation/getting-started-guide/string-translation/).

You will find many strings are already loaded into the String Translation table, ready for you to translate, include **Campaign Bar** and **admin_texts_theme_mods_konte**. They are 2 text domains that you will find texts from the Konte theme options.
