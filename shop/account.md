# My Account

**My Account** page presents information of logged in user. When user is not logged in, this page will show the login form. Konte also uses the login form of this page for the login panel.


## Registration Form

By default, you will see the login form only. If you want to have the registration form, you need to enable it in **WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy**.

![Enable registration form](_images/woocommerce-settings-account.png)


## Edit Registration Privacy Policy

On the registration form, you will a privacy policy. If you want to edit it, please navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings > Accounts & Privacy**.

![Edit registration privacy policy](_images/woocommerce-registration-policy.png)


## Remove an Item from Account Navigation

![Edit registration privacy policy](_images/woocommerce-account-dashboard.png)

If you want to remove an item from the account navigation on the "My Account" page, you just need to empty the option for that endpoint in **WooCommerce > Settings > Advance**. They are options in the **Account endpoints** section.

![Edit registration privacy policy](_images/woocommerce-account-endpoint-settings.png)
